## Import checklist

1. Preserve included source paths, .mtl files and referenced textures; selected model sources are unchanged.
2. Use Studio importer or a DCC conversion where needed; normalise to the guide bounds, floor-centre the pivot and author +Z forward.
3. Remove source decals/materials that do not match Pulse. One reused palette/trim material is preferred; preserve a pre-edit source copy.
4. Supply simple collision proxies and the named colour slots. Upload under the experience owner/group and verify permission in a test place.
5. Replace PENDING IDs in the register and UIAssets only after successful upload. Never pass placeholders to Roblox loaders.
6. Audition five cues at modest volume, compare loudness, then upload only approved files. No custom music or font is required.
7. Review the actual contact sheet and profile one finished theme before repeating across twelve plots.
